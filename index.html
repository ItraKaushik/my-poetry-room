Tera Itrana/तेरा इतराना

Hey jindgi tu kyun itna itrati hai, tinka-tinka bunti main, kyun har bar bikhra jaati hai, hey jingdi tu kyun itna itrati hai... Ek laghu samay antraal main, kala ghana tufaaan ched jaati hai, itna ghana! na dikhe kuchh, vo andhkaar bikher jaati hai, hey jingdi tu kyun itna itrati hai... Dikhta hai sahi par hota nahi, na naav kinare jaati hai, jo raah sahi uss mod leyi, par der badi ho jaati hai, hey jingdi tu kyun itna itrati hai... Itna khel chuke hum saath, nye khel khan se laati hai, yaa tu dhurt, ya main saral, teri chaal samajh nahi aati hai, hey jingdi tu kyun itna itrati hai... Ab jo sikh gai main uthna, tu jitni baar mujhe girati hai, chod de paryas karna, ab to dya tujhi pe aati hai, hey jingdi tu kyun itna itrati hai... Itra Kaushik
